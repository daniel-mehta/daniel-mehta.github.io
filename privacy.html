PRIVACY / SITE OPERATIONS

Privacy Policy

Effective August 8, 2026

OVERVIEWPERSONAL WEBSITE

This policy explains how Daniel Mehta's website handles information when you browse it or use its contact form. The website does not sell visitor information, serve advertising, or use cross-site behavioural tracking.

This is an informational policy for a personal website and is intended to support applicable Canadian privacy principles, including PIPEDA where it applies in Ontario. It is not a statement of legal certification.

AGGREGATE ANALYTICSGOATCOUNTER

This website uses hosted GoatCounter analytics to understand general site use and improve the website.

GoatCounter records aggregate statistics such as pages viewed, referring pages, browser and operating-system type, language, screen width, and country-level location. It does not use persistent cookies, local storage, stored IP addresses, stored full User-Agent headers, or a persistent visitor identifier for this website's analytics.

GoatCounter's hosted service processes data on servers in Finland and Germany and is operated from Ireland. Learn more in GoatCounter's privacy policy.

CONTACT FORMTYPEFORM

If you use the contact form, you choose what information to submit. That information is used to review and respond to your message.

The form is provided by Typeform, which processes submissions under its own privacy policy. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information through the form unless it is necessary.

YOUR CHOICESACCESS / QUESTIONS

You may use the site without submitting the contact form. If you have a privacy question, want to request access to or correction of personal information you submitted through the form, or want to raise a concern, use the contact form and identify your request as a privacy request.

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used for information under this website's control. You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada about privacy rights or concerns.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICYNOTICE

This policy may be updated when the website's information practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.
