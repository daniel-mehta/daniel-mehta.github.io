# Labels Are Not Ground Truth
> 2026-09-26

**Labels are not ground truth.**

Supervised learning may seem simple in the case where labels are available, but the labels might still include noise, inconsistencies, or subjective judgments. A model can just as easily pick up on those errors as it can on the useful signal.

The problem with unsupervised learning is that mathematical clusters can be distinct even if they do not have any real-world significance.

The bottom line is clear: when selecting a model, it is necessary to take into account the quality of the evidence and the particular **domain context**, not merely the fact that a target column is present.
