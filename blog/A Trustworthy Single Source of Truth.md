# Data Warehousing and a Trustworthy Single Source of Truth
> 2026-11-13

Does having all your data in one place automatically make it trustworthy?

Imagine moving every book from several libraries into one building. Everything is now centralised, but some books are outdated, duplicated, or incorrectly labelled.

Having everything in one place doesn't solve those problems.

The same challenge exists in data warehousing. Organisations often bring information from multiple systems together to establish a single source of truth (SSOT). However, centralisation alone doesn't guarantee accurate, consistent, or reliable data.

A trustworthy SSOT also requires shared definitions, clear responsibilities, and data quality controls that reflect how the information will be used.

Even decentralised approaches, such as data mesh, can support a reliable SSOT when departments follow common standards and governance policies.

Centralisation is an architectural choice. Trustworthiness is a governance and data quality outcome.
